# How I work

**Automating what matters.**

I approach workflows and projects with a versatile set of skills: process analysis, technical troubleshooting, workflow design, AI orchestration, and clear documentation. I draw on these skills to tackle unfamiliar problems, learn what is needed, and choose a solution that fits the situation.

My goal is to make things better. That means making a deliberate effort to simplify processes, reduce repetitive work, clarify ownership, and build systems that people can understand and maintain.

## My approach

1. **Understand the problem.** Listen to the people doing the work, map the current process, and identify where time, information, or ownership gets lost.
2. **Choose the right tools.** Combine the skills and systems the problem requires. Use AI where context or conversation adds value, and explicit logic for routing, validation, and record handling.
3. **Build and test.** Start with a focused workflow, check the normal path and failure cases, and document what has been validated and what still needs work.
4. **Improve and maintain.** Use feedback to refine the workflow, explain the decisions, and leave clear operating procedures for the people who will use it.

My IT support and enablement platform operations background keeps this approach practical: solve the underlying operational problem and make the next step clearer for the people involved.
